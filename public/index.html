An early ocean, seen through a neural lens

Over sixty unicellular organisms drift in a shallow primordial ocean. Green nutrient soup spreads across the upper photic zone, while deep-sea hydrothermal vents feed chemosynthetic life from below.

Each organism possesses an evolvable Mini Neural Network Brain mapping sensory inputs (food, prey, predators, kin, energy) directly to motor thrust, predation attacks, and colony adhesion. Brain weights and physical genes evolve across generations through asexual mitosis, sexual crossover, and local adaptive learning.

Organisms can form sticky colonies and transition into specialized multicellular creatures (feeders, defenders, reproducers, navigators). Click any organism to inspect its real-time neural network wiring diagrams, edit its genome, or trigger environmental disasters.
